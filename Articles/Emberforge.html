Emberforge

The sanctuary that refused to fall.

The War of Magic ended not with triumph, but with silence.

Great kingdoms had fallen. Cities were reduced to rubble. Civilization itself stood on the brink of extinction. Those who survived wandered a broken world where every blade was invaluable and every stranger a potential threat.

Among the remnants stood a lone survivor.

Few knew his past. Fewer still knew his name. Rumors spread among the scattered survivors of a craftsman capable of restoring shattered blades, mending broken armor, and forging equipment strong enough to withstand the horrors of the new age. In a world consumed by survival, he offered something greater: hope.

His name was Andre Emberforge, the Blacksmith.

Drawn by that hope, refugees began gathering around the blacksmith's humble forge. What began as a handful of scattered camps slowly grew into a thriving settlement. Hunters, merchants, healers, craftsmen, and wanderers all found purpose there, each contributing to the survival of the community.

In time, the settlement became known simply as Emberforge.

Not because it had always borne that name, but because its people had built their lives around the forge that kept them alive.

Even decades later, Emberforge remains a symbol of resilience. It is neither the greatest city nor the richest kingdom. It is a sanctuary forged through perseverance, where worn equipment is given new life, weary travelers find shelter, and every journey into the unknown begins beside the enduring glow of a single forge.
