Development Log

A week-by-week record of development for Lands of Tribulation™.

Entry 003 — August 11, 2026

Development continues with a major expansion to the gameplay systems of Lands of Tribulation™. We've been working behind the scenes on several additions that will directly impact combat, spells, and dungeon encounters. More details will be revealed throughout the week as these systems take shape.

Website / Studio

  • Developing a strategy to share development updates, insights, and behind-the-scenes content more regularly across all platforms.
  • Preparing a more consistent schedule for future announcements and development reveals.

Game Development

  • Began implementing elemental effects, expanding the game's combat and spell systems.
  • Individual elemental effects will be revealed throughout the week, with first announcements appearing on X.
  • A dedicated article covering the majority of the new elemental effects will be published in the Archives following the reveals.
  • Added buffs and debuffs to expand combat interactions and strategic possibilities.
  • Continued enhancing dungeon systems and encounters for more engaging gameplay.
  • Began implementing new VFX and SFX for spells to make abilities feel more impactful and responsive.

Looking Ahead

The introduction of elemental effects is only the beginning. Follow us on X throughout the week for the first reveals, and keep an eye on the Archives for a larger breakdown of the new systems.

More updates are coming. Stay tuned.

The journey continues. See you in the next entry.

Entry 002 — August 4, 2026

Development has continued steadily since the release of our first public demo. Much of this week's work focused on strengthening our public presence and preparing for the demo launch that took place on August 1, 2026, while also laying the foundation for the next stage of development. Every update brings Lands of Tribulation™ one step closer to the experience we envision.

Website / Studio

  • Redesigned sections of the homepage to create a stronger first impression.
  • Added a new Visit the Archives section to showcase the world's expanding lore.
  • Published our first lore article, "Emberforge", beginning the documentation of Tribulation's history.
  • Continued refining the website's presentation, layout, and navigation.

Game Development

  • Continued gathering feedback from the public demo.
  • Discussed improvements to the combat experience and overall gameplay flow.
  • Began planning the next wave of features and content following the demo release.
  • Continued polishing existing systems and addressing bugs identified during testing.

Looking Ahead

Our current focus is expanding the world of Lands of Tribulation™. In the coming weeks, players can expect additional lore, side-by-side comparison footage highlighting recent enhancements, and continued progress toward our Kickstarter campaign.
