About the Game

Lands of Tribulation is a dark fantasy turn-based RPG where every journey beyond the safety of civilization is a gamble.

A forgotten war gave birth to a corruption that transformed the land into a place ruled by monsters, demons, and desperate survivors. You begin not as a legendary hero, but as another refugee searching for sanctuary among the last remnants of humanity.

Master tactical combat, venture into dangerous lands, recover lost knowledge, and return alive. Every expedition strengthens not only your character, but the settlement that stands against the darkness.

From Survivor to Veteran

Every expedition leaves its mark. Progress from scavenged equipment to powerful armor forged from the dangers you've overcome. Survival is measured not by destiny, but by perseverance.

Prepare for the Unknown

Choose your equipment carefully before venturing beyond the settlement. Every weapon, piece of armor, and tool contributes to your chances of returning home.

Beyond the Walls

Beyond the safety of the settlement lies a fractured world filled with forgotten ruins, valuable resources, wandering monsters, and untold stories. Every journey into the wilderness is a calculated risk—and every safe return is a victory.

The World

War gave birth to darkness.

The lands beyond the settlement are consumed by corruption, where forgotten kingdoms lie buried beneath ash and monsters roam unchecked.

Every expedition uncovers another fragment of history... and another reason the world fell.
