Lands of Tribulation

A dark fantasy turn-based RPG where survival is earned—not given.

The world has already ended.

A forgotten war gave birth to a plague that twisted the land into monsters, demons, and desperate survivors. You are not the chosen one. You are another soul fleeing the ruins, searching for refuge in humanity's last surviving settlement.

Grow stronger. Uncover the past. Survive the Tribulation.

About the Game

Lands of Tribulation is a dark fantasy turn-based RPG where every journey beyond the safety of civilization is a gamble.

Master tactical combat, venture into dangerous lands, recover lost knowledge, and return alive. Every expedition strengthens not only your character, but the settlement that stands against the darkness.
