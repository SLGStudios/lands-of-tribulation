The Beginning

Before the darkness consumed the land, civilization flourished. Kingdoms expanded, knowledge was preserved, and humanity believed itself beyond the reach of ruin.

But peace was fragile.

The Forgotten War

No surviving record agrees on how the war began.

Some claim it was fought for power. Others claim it was fought against something humanity was never meant to awaken.

What remains certain is this: the war did not end with victory. It ended with survival.

Lands of Tribulation

The land was corrupted. The dead no longer remained silent. Creatures once thought impossible began appearing beyond the ruined borders of civilization.

Those who survived abandoned their kingdoms and fled toward the last remaining settlements.

The Last Settlement

Humanity's final hope is not a kingdom. It is a refuge.

A place where survivors gather knowledge, repair what they can, and prepare for another journey into the darkness.

The settlement does not promise safety. Only another chance.
